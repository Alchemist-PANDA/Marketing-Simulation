# Dataset Card — `expanded_real_dataset.csv`

> ⚠️ **This is a SYNTHETIC development benchmark, not real-world ad data.**
> The filename `expanded_real_dataset.csv` matches the pipeline's expected path;
> "real" here means "full-scale," **not** "collected from real campaigns."

## Why synthetic?

A genuine search was run for a real, large English ad-text + CTR dataset
(Hugging Face Hub queried for `ad ctr`, `advertising ctr`, `ad creative`,
`ctr prediction`, `facebook ads`, `ad copy`; Kaggle requires credentials not
available in this environment). Findings:

| Candidate | Has ad *text*? | Has real *CTR*? | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `EvgeniaKyriazi/ctr-prediction-dataset` (Avazu) | ❌ anonymized hashes | ✅ `click` | No text — unusable for a semantic model |
| `PeterBrendan/Ads_Creative_Ad_Copy_Programmatic` | ✅ real ad copy | ❌ none | No labels |
| `ma-zn/*avito*ad-ctr` | ⚠️ Russian DB metadata | ⚠️ relevance score | Non-English, not ad creative |

**Conclusion:** no public dataset pairs English ad *creative text* with real CTR
at ≥2,000 scale. Datasets with CTR have no text; datasets with text have no CTR.
So this benchmark is generated by `src/ai/synth_data.py`.

## Contents

- **Rows:** 2,600 unique ads
- **Split:** 70% train (1,819) / 15% val (390) / 15% holdout (391), seed = 42
- **Columns:** `ad_text`, `industry`, `actual_ctr`, `impressions`,
  `factor_urgency`, `factor_social`, `factor_spec`, `factor_cta`,
  `factor_curiosity`, `factor_price`
- **Industries:** 20 (fashion, electronics, SaaS, fintech, health, beauty, …),
  each with a plausible base CTR and a representative product noun written into
  the ad text.

## Generative model (documented, so accuracy is interpretable)

`actual_ctr = base(industry) × quality(text factors) × noise`

- `base(industry)`: 0.006–0.028, inferable from the product noun in the text.
- `quality`: weighted non-linear combination of urgency, social proof,
  specificity, CTA strength, curiosity, and price framing, **including two
  interaction terms** (urgency×CTA, specificity×social). Each factor is
  expressed through *families of interchangeable phrases*, so a model must
  generalize semantically rather than memorize keywords.
- `noise`: multiplicative log-normal, σ = 0.07 — caps achievable accuracy
  below 100%.

## Honest interpretation

Accuracy on this data measures how well the pipeline **recovers a known
generative function from text**. It is a software/regression benchmark. It is
**not** evidence of real-world CTR-prediction accuracy, which would require real
campaign data to establish.
