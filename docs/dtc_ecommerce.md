# Pre-Flight Testing for DTC E-Commerce Brands

The Marketing Simulation Engine is purpose-built as a **digital wind tunnel for Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) E-commerce brands**. 

Before you spend thousands of dollars on Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok ads to find a winning creative or offer, you can run your assets through our simulation to get deterministic, psychographically modeled directional feedback.

## Why DTC E-Commerce?
DTC brands face brutal Customer Acquisition Costs (CAC) and rely heavily on rapid creative testing. We've optimized this engine to help you answer questions like:
- *Will this "50% Off" offer convert better than "Buy 1 Get 1 Free" for our budget-conscious segment?*
- *Is this ad copy relying too much on urgency and destroying trust with our premium audience?*
- *Which of these 10 user-generated content (UGC) ad hooks has the highest probability of catching attention?*

## Specialized DTC Features

### 1. Offer Type Modeling
Different offers trigger different psychological responses under Prospect Theory. The engine allows you to select:
- **Discount (%)**: High perceived value, triggers loss aversion if limited time.
- **BOGO (Buy One Get One)**: Appeals strongly to budget-conscious and high-openness traits.
- **Free Shipping**: Often the highest conversion driver due to the removal of friction.
- **Bundle**: High average order value (AOV) but requires higher trust.

### 2. Customer Segment Presets
Instead of a generic 1M agent pool, simulate against:
- **Budget-Conscious**: Highly price elastic, strong loss aversion.
- **Premium/Luxury**: Low price elasticity, demands high trust and social proof.
- **Early Adopters**: High openness, low neuroticism, willing to try new products.
- **General Broad**: The default Meta Ads "Broad Targeting" pool.

### 3. Price Elasticity Testing
Input your product price to see how the perceived value aligns with the ad copy's promises. High price + low trust copy = simulation failure.

## The Workflow
1. **Ideate**: Write 5-10 ad variants or offers.
2. **Simulate**: Run them through the Multi-Ad Experimentation flow.
3. **Analyze**: Use the AI Recommendation Engine to fix the losers.
4. **Deploy**: Take the simulation winner and put real budget behind it on Meta/TikTok.
