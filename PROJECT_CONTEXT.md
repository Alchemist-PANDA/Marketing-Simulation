# Advanced Marketing Simulation Engine (MaxCap)

## Purpose
A digital wind tunnel for marketing campaigns, using high-fidelity agent simulations to predict real-world engagement, conversion, and word-of-mouth spread before spending a single dollar on real ads.

## Core Components
- **Advanced Agent Brain**: Agents are driven by Big Five personality traits (Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, Neuroticism) and behavioral economics (Prospect Theory, Hyperbolic Discounting).
- **Market Dynamics Engine**: Models seasonality (holidays), network effects (Metcalfe's Law), and speculative bubble detection.
- **CLV & Churn Analytics**: Probabilistic Customer Lifetime Value modeling and ROI-based churn intervention recommendations.
- **Strategic AI (Game Theory)**: Nash Equilibrium and Tit-for-Tat models for competitive brand analysis and price war simulations.
- **SQLite Analytics**: Every simulation event is logged for granular segment analysis and ROI tracking.

## Key Goals
1. **Budget Protection**: Prevent loss of marketing budget by pre-validating ad variants.
2. **Calibration**: Achieve high simulation-to-reality accuracy through real-world A/B test alignment.
3. **Forensic Feedback**: Understand *why* an ad failed (e.g., "price too high", "channel untrustworthy").
